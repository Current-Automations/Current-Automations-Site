# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Owner-operated trades businesses (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, cleaning, landscaping) in Durham Region + GTA, remote across Ontario. A 45-year-old owner who answers his own phone, is skeptical of tech, and responds to clarity over cleverness. He lands here from cold email or a call and decides in seconds whether this is real or another agency pitch.

## Product Purpose
Sell one front-door offer: a free audit that finds where the business leaks revenue (missed calls, slow follow-up, manual admin, intake bottlenecks). The site's job is to get the audit booked. Missed-call recovery is the flagship symptom, not the whole story. Success = "Book Free Audit" clicks and demo-line calls.

One front door for all four pillars. Lead generation is a different audience on a different pricing track, but its scoping call is the same free 30-minute audit and the same "Book Free Audit" CTA. Do not invent a second booking flow for it.

## The Four Pillars

The offering is four pillars. Identity is claimed by capability and intent, not by how built out each one is; build depth belongs in honest fine print, not in the branding. Each has its own page and its own demo video.

| # | Pillar | Page | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Call & dispatch handling | `/call-dispatch` | Missed-call text back, speed to lead, intake, booking. Every call, form and voicemail answered in seconds. |
| 02 | Auto-replies & follow-up | `/follow-up` | Reply and follow-up sequences that keep quotes moving to a yes. Quote chasing, no-show recovery, database reactivation. |
| 03 | Back-office & admin | `/back-office` | The desk work that eats evenings. Invoice and payment follow-up first, more to come. |
| 04 | Lead generation | `/lead-generation` | B2B only. Pipelines that find and qualify decision-makers matching an exact ICP. |

**Pillar 04 is deliberately not one of the others.** The first three are trades lanes sold to the owner-operator described above. Lead generation is B2B, sold to corporate, construction-management and B2B service companies, on its own pricing track. It kept reading as a sore thumb inside the lane grid, so the design now says the difference out loud instead of managing it: on the homepage it sits below the three-lane grid on its own row, in a rust-bordered card with a "B2B only" stamp. Same scale, different paper. Do not fold it back into the grid for visual tidiness.

Pillar 03 is the newest and thinnest. Say so plainly ("First up: invoice and payment follow-up. More on the way") rather than implying a full back-office suite exists.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, direct, quietly confident. Industrial-tool-brand restraint (Basecamp/Shopify in directness, not in look). Plain language, concrete numbers, no jargon. Never leads with "AI"; leads with jobs won and calls answered.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI-agency landing pages (gradient-text hero, hype copy, fake urgency)
- Fake testimonials or invented social proof: banned outright
- SaaS hero-metric template and identical icon-card grids
- Anything that reads "tech startup" instead of "a guy who fixes a real problem for trades"

## Design Principles
- Proof you can dial: the live demo line (1-365-601-7474) is the strongest honest proof; surface it like a product
- Show the flow, don't claim the outcome: missed call → text in seconds → booked job, made visible
- One front door: every page funnels to "Book Free Audit"
- Premium through craft, not noise: depth, rhythm, and typography inside the existing Job Sheet paper/ticket system (`DESIGN.md`); teal stays scarce
- Write for the truck, not the boardroom

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast everywhere. Visible :focus-visible (2px teal) on all interactive elements. prefers-reduced-motion honored on every animation. Tap-to-call on mobile.
